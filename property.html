The Cottage

Three bedrooms • Two bathrooms • Sleeps six • Dogs welcome

Bron Heulog is a well-loved, well-equipped Welsh cottage that sits quietly in the hills just outside Maenaddwyn on Anglesey. The sunroom sits on a slightly different level to the rest of the house, and there are three steps to the entrance. Otherwise it has been fitted out with everything a group of six could need — and one or two things they won’t be expecting.

Living Room & Dining

The living room is the heart of Bron Heulog. A cast-iron wood-burning stove sits at its centre, with generous seating for all six guests arranged around it. On darker evenings there’s nowhere better to be.

The dining area has a table for six and a Smart TV. A set of sliding doors opens through to the sunroom, and beyond that the patio — so on warmer days the inside and outside blur pleasantly into one another.

  • Wood-burning stove
  • Seating for six
  • Smart TV
  • Dining table for six
  • Sunroom with terrace access
  • Views across Anglesey

The Sunroom

Perhaps the most-loved room in the cottage. The sunroom is all glass and light, with uninterrupted views across Anglesey’s patchwork of fields and hedgerows toward the peaks of Snowdonia. On a clear morning, with a mug of coffee in hand, it’s extraordinary.

Double doors lead out to the paved patio with seating and a charcoal barbecue, making this the natural hub of the cottage through the warmer months.

  • Full-length glazing on three sides
  • Views toward Snowdonia
  • Direct access to patio terrace
  • Seating for six on terrace
  • Charcoal BBQ (fuel not supplied)

Kitchen

The kitchen is properly equipped for a group holiday. An electric hob, double oven and microwave handle the cooking; a dishwasher and coffee machine handle the rest. There’s a full-size fridge in the kitchen, and a fridge freezer in the utility room just off the hallway — plenty of space for a week’s worth of supplies.

  • Electric hob (4-ring)
  • Double oven
  • Microwave
  • Dishwasher
  • Coffee machine
  • Full-size fridge
  • Utility room: fridge freezer & washer-dryer

Bedrooms

Three bedrooms sleeping six, with the master bedroom having its own ensuite.

Bedroom One — Double Ensuite

The master bedroom has a comfortable double bed and its own ensuite shower room, giving the party a little extra privacy if needed. Light and airy with countryside views from the window.

  • Double bed
  • Ensuite shower room
  • Countryside views

Bedrooms Two & Three

The second bedroom has a comfortable double bed. The third bedroom is a twin, with two single beds — ideal for children or friends travelling together. All bedrooms have linen and towels provided.

  • Bedroom 2: double bed
  • Bedroom 3: twin beds (two singles)
  • Linen and towels included
  • Travel cot available on request
  • High chair available on request

Bathrooms

There are two bathrooms at Bron Heulog. The family bathroom has a Jacuzzi bath — one of the most appreciated features of the cottage after a long day walking the Anglesey coastal path or exploring the beaches. There is also a heated towel radiator, WC and sink.

The ensuite (off the master bedroom) has a walk-in shower, WC, sink and heated towel radiator.

  • Family bathroom: Jacuzzi bath, WC, sink, heated towel rail
  • Ensuite: walk-in shower, WC, sink, heated towel rail
  • Bath towels provided

Garden & Outside Space

The cottage sits in a mostly gravelled and terraced garden. The patio terrace directly off the sunroom has outdoor seating for six and a charcoal barbecue.

Off-road parking for up to four cars. An EV charger is available (app-payable).

  • Patio seating for six
  • Charcoal BBQ (fuel not supplied)
  • Off-road parking for 4 cars
  • EV charger (app-payable)
